## Ticket Goal #5 — Wood Badge Jamboree at Lost Pines

*  **Statement:** Host a short-term camp at Lost Pines with climbing, shooting, and swimming for Scouts from small and new units, so their leaders can attend Wood Badge without their units missing a campout in prime camping season. When Julia attended Wood Badge, she missed the unit campout between course weekends, the only one she missed in her tenure. If a leader that committed has to miss a campout to attend, leaders in small units, who have no backup, often skip Wood Badge entirely; this camp removes that trade-off.

    ### Specific

    >| | |
    >| --- | --- |
    >| What | A staffed weekend camp running during the second weekend of a Wood Badge course. |
    >| Why | Small/new units can't spare their only leaders for two weekends, so those leaders skip Wood Badge. |
    >| Who | Me as climbing instructor. Timmie as camp director and second tower instructor, certified range/aquatics staff recruited through my contacts, volunteer adults, Scouts from small units; their leaders transport Scouts in and out. |
    >| Where | Lost Pines Scout Reservation. |
    >| Which | Council short-term camp approval, certified RSO, climbing instructors, lifeguard and aquatics supervision, two-deep leadership/YPT, site reservation, fees. |

    ### **Measurable**
    * One camp held; 1–3 units attend; at least 1 leader attends Wood Badge because of it.
    * Done when the camp runs and attendance is reported to the course director.

    ### **Attainable**
    * Roadblocks: certified program staff, council approval, liability, cost.
        * Overcome by recruiting certified staff through my personal network, partnering with the council camping committee early, and setting a fee that covers program cost.

    ### **Relevant**
    * Removes a barrier to adult training in small units — trained leaders deliver better programs.

    ### **Timely**
    * Today: confirm next Wood Badge course dates and Lost Pines availability.
    * 12 weeks: council approval path and staff recruiting.
    * 5 months: site booked, staff committed, registration open.
    * Camp runs on the second weekend of the 2027 course.

    >| | | |
    >| --- | --- | --- |
    >| **Diversity** | Yes | reaches small and new units that are usually underserved. |
    >| **Membership growth/retention** | Yes | trained leaders retain youth; new units stabilize. |
    >| **Line of sight to youth** | Yes | youth get a program weekend; their leaders return trained. |
